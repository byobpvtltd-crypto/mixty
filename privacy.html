Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains how Mixty ("we", "us"), Khadki, Pune 411003, India, handles your information when you order food through us on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Messages, cart screenshots and payment screenshots you send us.
  • Your delivery address or shared location.
  • Order details: items, prices, payment reference numbers, delivery status and any feedback.

How we use it

  • To read your cart, prepare a price quote and place your order with the restaurant.
  • To arrange delivery and send you order and tracking updates.
  • To verify payments, issue refunds and keep accounting records.
  • To remember your address and past orders so repeat orders are faster.

Who we share it with

Only what is needed to complete your order:

  • The restaurant: the items you ordered.
  • The delivery partner (e.g. Porter): your name, phone number and delivery address.
  • WhatsApp / Meta: messages are exchanged through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • An automated text-recognition service, used to read the items and prices in your cart screenshots.

We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, except where we are required by law to keep it.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete your information, or to stop receiving messages, send "STOP" or your request on WhatsApp, or email sunkisardar@gmail.com.
