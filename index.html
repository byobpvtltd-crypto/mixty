Large order for a family dinner, a party or the office? Send us your cart on WhatsApp. We order it directly from the restaurant and get it delivered to your door, usually for less than you'd pay through a food delivery app.

How it works

  1. Build your cart in any food app as usual, then send us a screenshot on WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm the items with the restaurant and send you one total price, including delivery.
  3. Approve and pay by UPI.
  4. We place the order directly with the restaurant and book a delivery partner. You get live tracking on WhatsApp.

Minimum order value ₹1,500. Currently serving Pune.
