The service

Mixty takes food orders on WhatsApp, places them directly with restaurants in Pune and arranges delivery through a third-party delivery partner. The minimum order value is ₹1,500.

Prices and payment

  • Before you pay, we send one total price covering food, delivery and our service fee.
  • You confirm the order by replying YES and paying the quoted amount to us by UPI.
  • We place the order with the restaurant only after your payment is verified.

Cancellations and refunds

  • You can cancel free of charge any time before you pay.
  • If we cannot fulfil a paid order (for example, the restaurant is closed or an item is unavailable), we refund the full amount to your UPI account.
  • Once the restaurant has started preparing your order, it can no longer be cancelled.
  • If items are missing or wrong, tell us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery and we will arrange a fair refund.

Food quality

Food is prepared by the restaurant. Allergen and ingredient questions should be checked before ordering; we will pass on your questions to the restaurant on request.
